# Medibit

**An automatic pill dispenser that helps elderly people take the right medicine on time.**<br>
A micro:bit drives a DC motor that pulls a slider under the pill compartments, with an LED and a
buzzer as the reminder. Built for the InnoBit Challenge 2019 by a team from the Victoria Robotics and
Makers Club (VRMC), Victoria Junior College, and placed in the top 10.

## The problem

Singapore's share of elderly people was set to rise from 8.4% in 2005 to 18.7% in 2030. Many take
many medications (the team cites up to 20), and missed or avoided doses let chronic conditions
go on. The question: how can we improve medication adherence in the elderly?

## How it works

1. Pills sit in compartments.
2. A slider beneath the compartments releases the pills when it moves.
3. A DC motor pulls the slider by a string.
4. A micro:bit controls the motor, an LED and a buzzer, so the dose comes with a reminder.
